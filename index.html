About Us

Fountain House of Food is a leading food manufacturing company based in Obio Akpor, Rivers State. We are committed to producing high-quality food products that meet international standards while celebrating Nigerian flavors and traditions. Our state-of-the-art facility at Rumuapu Apu Road, Rukpokwu, is equipped with modern machinery and operated by skilled professionals dedicated to food safety and excellence. We pride ourselves on being a reliable partner for businesses and consumers across Nigeria, delivering products that nourish families and support communities.
