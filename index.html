About Us

Steve Chy Subsea Engineering Co (also operating as Steve Chy Offshore Solutions Limited) is a leading Nigerian oil and gas engineering firm specializing in subsea systems and reservoir engineering. Led by experienced reservoir engineers, we combine technical expertise with local knowledge to deliver cost-effective, safe, and sustainable solutions for offshore operations.

Our commitment to excellence and understanding of Nigeria's unique oil and gas landscape makes us the preferred partner for operators seeking reliable subsea engineering services. We maintain the highest industry standards while providing responsive, client-focused service throughout Obio Akpor and the broader Niger Delta region.

Our Services

Subsea Engineering

Comprehensive subsea system design, installation supervision, and integrity management for offshore facilities. We provide technical support for subsea infrastructure including pipelines, manifolds, and flowlines.

Reservoir Engineering

Expert reservoir analysis, production optimization, and field development planning. Our team delivers detailed reservoir characterization and performance forecasting to maximize asset value.

Offshore Solutions

Complete offshore engineering support including platform engineering, mooring systems analysis, and marine operations planning tailored to Nigerian offshore conditions.

Technical Consulting

Strategic advisory services for oil and gas operators, covering feasibility studies, risk assessment, and regulatory compliance for offshore projects.

Project Management

End-to-end project execution support ensuring safe, on-time, and budget-conscious delivery of subsea and offshore engineering projects.
